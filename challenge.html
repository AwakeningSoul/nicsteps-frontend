YOUR 28 DAY JOURNEY

How it works.

Four weeks. One step at a time. Progress at your own pace.

01

Choose your target

Register and select a daily step target that feels right for you.

i

Choose from our daily step targets and pick one that feels achievable for you. You can challenge yourself, but remember that consistency matters more than speed.

02

Keep moving

Work towards your daily target and build healthy movement habits.

i

Fit your steps into everyday life. Walk to the shops, take the stairs, go for a stroll or enjoy a longer walk. Every step counts towards your daily target.

03

Report each week

Submit your seven daily step totals and activity evidence each week.

i

You can use any suitable step-tracking device or app, including Fitbit, Apple Health, Google Fit, Samsung Health or your phone's built-in step counter.

When submitting your weekly report, upload clear screenshots showing your recorded steps as evidence. You don't need a Fitbit — any suitable tracker is welcome, as long as it provides clear evidence of your activity.

04

Celebrate progress

Earn points for meeting your target and follow the verified leaderboard.

i

Your verified weekly reports earn points based on how consistently you meet your daily target. Follow the leaderboard to see your progress and celebrate your achievements.
