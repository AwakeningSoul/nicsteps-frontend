Returns & Refunds

Accepted Returns

We accept returns for damaged items, misprints, or incorrect items. Contact us within 7 days of delivery.

Not Accepted

We do not accept returns for wrong size ordered, change of mind, or colour differences.

Refunds

If approved, refunds are issued via Stripe to your original payment method.

Replacements

Printful will remake defective items at no cost.

Lost Packages

If tracking is missing, we will investigate with Printful.
